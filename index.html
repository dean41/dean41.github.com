Games · 游戏作品

Flowerz · 荷塘春色

A premium, ad-free puzzle game. Plant the flowers the queue hands you onto a 7×7 pond board and clear them in lines of three or more. No gravity refill, no timers, no luck — the four-piece preview makes every move predictable. 20 hand-built levels, two-colour flowers, plus a dragonfly and a shovel as helpers.

一款付费买断、无广告的益智拼图游戏。把队列送来的花种进 7×7 的荷塘棋盘，横竖连成三个或更多同色即消除 —— 没有重力补位、没有计时、不靠运气，4 格预览让每一步都可预判。20 个手工关卡，双色花，外加蜻蜓与铲子两种道具。

Privacy · 隐私

Our games collect no personal data. Flowerz has no account system, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests at runtime. Progress, scores, and settings are stored on your device only, and are removed when you delete the app.

我们的游戏不收集任何个人数据。《荷塘春色》没有账号系统、没有广告、没有统计分析、也没有接入任何第三方 SDK，运行时亦不发起网络请求。进度、分数与设置仅保存在你的设备本地，删除应用即一并清除。
