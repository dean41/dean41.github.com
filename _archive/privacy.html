2. Accounts, Game Center and Leaderboards

[APP_NAME] does not require registration, login, or an account of our own. You can use the App immediately after installing it. We never ask for your name, email address, or password, and we operate no servers that store player profiles.

2.1 Game Center (iOS only, optional)

On iOS you may optionally sign in with Apple Game Center to submit scores to leaderboards and unlock achievements. Game Center is an Apple service: authentication happens entirely through your Apple ID, and we never see or store your Apple ID credentials.

If you choose to sign in, the following is processed:

  • Game Center player ID — a persistent identifier generated by Apple;
  • Leaderboard scores and achievement progress.

This data is stored by Apple on Game Center's servers and is cached locally on your device. We do not operate our own leaderboard server and do not copy your scores anywhere else. We do not use your Game Center player ID for tracking, advertising, or analytics, and we do not share it with any third party.

Signing in is entirely optional — declining does not restrict any other part of the App. On Android the App does not upload scores at all; scores are kept only on your device.

2.2 Local data

Your settings and best scores are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is erased when you uninstall the App.

Advertising identifiers described in §3 are generated by your device's operating system and are not linked by us to your real-world identity.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information collected automatically or via Game Center

  • Advertising identifier: your device's IDFA (iOS) or Advertising ID / AAID (Android), used to deliver and measure ads.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, screen size, language, time zone, app version.
  • Usage data: screens viewed, features used, session duration, and interactions with ads.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance logs.
  • Game Center data (iOS only, optional): player ID, leaderboard scores, and achievement progress — collected only if you sign in. See §2.1.

3.2 Information you provide

  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to reply.
  • Purchase data: handled entirely by Apple or Google. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded — never your card details.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob is Google's advertising platform and it collects and processes the data listed in §3 in order to select, deliver, and measure ads.

4.1 How Google uses this data

Google and its advertising partners may use device identifiers, cookies, and similar technologies to serve personalised ads — ads selected based on interests inferred from your app usage. Google may also use this data to limit how often you see the same ad, to detect invalid traffic and ad fraud, and to produce aggregated reporting for us.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms of Service. Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4.2 Your choices

  • Opt out of personalised ads (iOS): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" for [APP_NAME].
  • Opt out of personalised ads (Android): Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of ads personalisation.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising; Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
  • Industry opt-out tools: aboutads.info/choices or networkadvertising.org/choices.

Opting out does not remove ads — you will still see them, but they will be less relevant. Opting out does not delete data Google has already collected; use the procedure in §9 for that.

4.3 Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are served, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified Consent Management Platform. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from Settings → Privacy in the App. If you decline, only non-personalised (contextual) ads are shown.

4.4 Your California rights (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under California law, the use of advertising identifiers for cross-context behavioural advertising may be treated as "sale" or "sharing". California residents may opt out from Settings → Privacy → Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information in the App, or by emailing us.
