[APP_NAME] has no accounts, no login, and no registration, so there is no user profile stored under your name. We keep no copy of your scores. Leaderboard data lives on Apple's servers and on your device. We operate no game server of our own, so there is nothing on our side to delete — the steps below remove it at the source.

Method 1 — Reset your advertising ID (fastest)

iOS

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  3. Turn on Personalized Ads off, or reset your Advertising Identifier.

Android

  1. Open Settings → Google → Ads.
  2. Tap Reset advertising ID, then confirm.
  3. Optionally enable Opt out of ads personalisation.

This immediately severs the link between your device and ad data collected previously. Uninstalling the App has the same effect on locally stored preferences.

Method 2 — Remove leaderboard scores and local data

Local settings and best scores

Uninstall [APP_NAME]. Everything saved on your device — settings, progress, best scores — is erased with it.

Game Center scores (iOS)

  1. Open Settings → Game Center.
  2. Sign out, or turn off the leaderboard for [APP_NAME].
  3. To delete data Apple already holds, go to privacy.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

We hold no copy of your scores, so there is nothing further to remove on our side.

On Android, scores are never uploaded — uninstalling the App removes everything.

Method 3 — Email us a deletion request

Ask us to delete what we hold

Email YeehawStudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • Your device platform (iOS or Android)
  • Your advertising ID (optional — helps us locate the records faster)

Email deletion request

We confirm by reply and complete deletion within 30 days.

  • Support correspondence you have sent us
