Privacy Policy

App: Flowerz · Last updated: September 22, 2026

Flowerz does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests at runtime. We operate no servers for this app, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

2. What stays on your device

To make the game work, the app saves the following locally on your device only:

DataWhy
Level progress and save slotsSo you can resume a game
Scores, achievements, play timeSo the personal stats and local leaderboard work
Settings (sound, music, language)So your preferences persist

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app.

3. Sharing

We share nothing, because we receive nothing. No data is disclosed to advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party — there are none in the app.

4. The in-game leaderboard

The leaderboard in Flowerz is local only. It ranks your own cleared runs on your own device. It does not use any platform game service (such as Apple Game Center or Google Play Games), it does not require an Apple ID or a Google account, and it does not upload anything.

5. Children's privacy

Flowerz is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no external links to social features.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over, correct, or erase on request. To remove all locally stored data, simply delete the app from your device — or use the in-game reset option to clear progress and settings while keeping the app installed.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. We recommend checking this page after major updates.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: YeehawStudio@gmail.com
